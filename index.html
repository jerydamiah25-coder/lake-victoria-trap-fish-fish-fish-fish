Lake Victoria · Nile perch control

A smart balloon trap that only keeps the invaders

Frozen white Nile tilapia sits inside a clear tube on top of a holding cage, the one bait Nile perch go for. An AI camera names each one. Nile perch get sealed in by pink balloons and pushed down into the cage. Native cichlids swim straight out the other end. Watch what happens to the lake over time, then switch the trap off to compare.

How the trap works

Waiting for a fish
  1. Bait draws a fish, balloon deflatesFrozen white tilapia in the tube pulls Nile perch in. Other fish sometimes follow the light. The bottom balloon shrinks to let it into the tube.
  2. AI camera identifies itThe camera reads the fish's shape and colours and names the species.
  3. Invasive: trap and pushBoth gate balloons inflate, the floor balloon opens, and the top balloon pushes the fish into the cage.
  4. Native: swim throughGates stay down and the floor balloon stays up, so the fish can't wander into the cage. It exits safely.

      Breeding patterns

      January · dry season

      Shaded months are the rainy seasons: long rains March to May, short rains October to December. Most fish breed more when it rains.

      SpeciesHow it breedsWhenIn the lake now

      Only grown females breed. Young fish are easy prey, so most don't survive. Brooding mothers show a swollen throat in the lake view, tilapia nests appear on the bottom, and perch eggs drift up as white specks. Growth and clutch sizes are scaled down: a year passes in about 6 minutes at 8×.

      Nile perch vs. cichlids, by day

      Nile perch (invasive) Haplochromine cichlids (native) Rainy seasons

      Nile perch hunt the small cichlids. When perch numbers climb, cichlids fall. With the baited trap on, perch stay low and cichlids recover. Turn the trap or the bait off to see the other path.

      A teaching model, not a field forecast. Nile perch were introduced to Lake Victoria in the 1950s and helped wipe out hundreds of haplochromine cichlid species. Fish counts, growth and predation rates here are simplified so the effect shows up in a few minutes.
